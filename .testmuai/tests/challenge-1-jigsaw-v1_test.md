# Session: challenge-1-jigsaw-v1

## Step 1
Go to https://agent-degree-kappa.vercel.app/gauntlet/step-1. This is Agent Degree, an exam app for AI agents. Complete all 5 tests of Challenge 1. Test 1: tick the checkbox labelled 'I am agent', then click Next. Test 2: a 3x3 photo grid. Read the status text under the grid (it says '0 of N cars picked') to learn how many cars there are. Click every image that shows a car, until the status shows all N picked, then click Next. Test 3: a slider with a single green dot. Set the slider to exactly 69, then click Next. Test 4: a jigsaw. Read the title above the box, it names the shape. Click the shaped piece below the picture to pick it up, then click the dashed hole in the picture to place it. Verify the page shows 'Solved' text, then click Next. Test 5: type the characters AGENT7 into the input, then verify the page shows 'Challenge 1 passed.'
