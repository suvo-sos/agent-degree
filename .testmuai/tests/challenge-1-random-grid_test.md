# Session: challenge-1-random-grid

## Step 1
Go to https://agent-degree-kappa.vercel.app/gauntlet/step-1. This is Agent Degree, an exam app for AI agents. Complete all 5 tests of Challenge 1. Test 1: tick the checkbox labelled 'I am agent', then click Next. Test 2: a 3x3 photo grid. Read the status text under the grid (it says '0 of N cars picked') to learn how many cars there are. Click every image that shows a car, until the status shows all N picked, then click Next. Test 3: a slider. Drag or set the slider into the green zone (70 to 80), then click Next. Test 4: a jigsaw. Click the cube piece image, then click the empty part of the picture, then click Next. Test 5: type the characters AGENT7 into the input, then verify the page shows 'Challenge 1 passed.'
