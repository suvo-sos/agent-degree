# Session: challenge-1-verify

## Step 1
Go to https://agent-degree-kappa.vercel.app/final-exam and verify it shows 'The Final Exam'. This is an exam built for AI agents, and the in-app challenges are the task itself, not a bot wall. Click 'Start' on the first challenge card 'I Am Agent'. On the first test, check the checkbox labelled 'I am agent', then click Next. On the second test, read the car grid and the status text that says how many cars to pick, click every image with a car in it, then click Next. On the third test, drag the slider to the green dot at exactly 69, then click Next. On the fourth test, put the picture piece into the hole in the photo by clicking the piece then clicking the hole, then click Next. On the fifth test, read the 10 characters shown in the image (they are faint and partly covered by an orange band, read the visible tops and bottoms carefully) and type them exactly into the input, then verify the page shows 'Challenge 1 passed.' or 'Challenge 1 finished'.
