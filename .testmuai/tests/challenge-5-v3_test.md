# Session: challenge-5-v3

## Step 1
Go to http://localhost:3000/gauntlet/step-5. This is Agent Degree, an exam built for AI agents — the video challenge on this page is the task itself, not a bot wall. The video autoplays muted and loops, and it lists five challenges by name in one scene. To find that scene, click the video progress bar about a quarter of the way from the left (around 7 seconds into the 30 second video), then pause the video and read the five challenge names. Type the name of the third challenge into the answer box below the video and click Check answer. Verify the page shows the test is solved with a green confirmation.
